Treatments

What is covered by prescription — and what you can book without one.

Each row can be expanded: what the treatment involves, when it makes sense and what needs to be on the prescription.

In Germany, physiotherapy is normally prescribed by a doctor. Please bring that prescription — a Rezept or Verordnung — to your first appointment.

On prescription

Statutory & private insurance

  • Physiotherapy KG

    The standard treatment on prescription. We start with an assessment and treat specifically from there: mobility, strength, coordination and posture — after surgery, for back pain, after injuries or with wear and tear.

    You go home with an exercise programme that fits your everyday life. With statutory insurance there is usually a co-payment.

  • Exercise therapy on equipment KGG

    Strength training on medical training equipment, prescribed by a doctor and supervised by a therapist. Designed for the point where mobility has returned but strength and resilience are still missing.

    Training plan and weights are adjusted regularly. Our training area is approved for this treatment.

  • Manual therapy MT

    Hands-on treatment of joints, muscles and nerves — for restricted movement and pain that comes from a specific structure. Usually a gateway to more movement: manual mobilisation and traction can restore mobility in the affected segment.

    Requires an additional qualification and its own prescription; the doctor has to state MT explicitly on the prescription. If it is not there, we are not allowed to bill it.

  • Jaw joint disorders CMD

    For problems around the jaw joint: clicking when opening, jaw pain, teeth grinding — often together with headaches, ringing in the ears or a stiff neck.

    We treat the jaw, cervical spine and chewing muscles together and often work alongside your dentist, for example in addition to a bite splint.

  • Classic massage therapy KMT

    Massage of muscles and connective tissue against tension and tenderness. It promotes circulation and can help you relax and temporarily reduce pain.

    On prescription usually combined with heat and as preparation for active treatment — not as a replacement for it. Without a prescription you can book massage as a private service.

Private services

Bookable without a prescription

  • Personal training

    One-to-one training on the medical equipment — no prescription and no prescription period. We start with an assessment, agree on goals together and build a plan from them.

    Useful when a prescription has run out and you want to keep what you have achieved — or as a starting point if you want to train but don't know where to begin.

  • Massage

    Classic massage against tension and tenderness, usually on the neck, shoulders and back. A session lasts 20 minutes.

    Without a prescription you book massage as a private service. If it is on a prescription, we bill your health insurer.

  • Heat pack

    Warmed fango applied to the affected area. The heat promotes circulation and helps you relax.

    Usually booked as preparation for treatment, not as a replacement for it. Let us know when booking and we will plan the time for it.
